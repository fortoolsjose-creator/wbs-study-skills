# Team AI map

First group activity of the workshop series: bring each member's experience with AI and attitude toward it to the surface, using the Week 2 AI survey results as a starting point.

## Per member (each person answers in their own words)

| Member | Tools used so far | What I use AI for | What I never use it for | How much I trust it (1-5) and why | A time it was wrong or misleading | What I want from the team's AI rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

## Team patterns

- Where do we agree?
- Where do we differ most?
- Which differences could create friction on the report (for example, one member drafts with AI and another does not)?

## Team agreements (draft, then confirm in the workshop)

1. Which steps of the project may involve AI?
2. Who checks citations at the source, and how do we record it?
3. Where do we keep each member's AI log and how do we merge it into one declaration?
4. How do we handle disagreement about AI use?
5. What check-in lets us review these rules at each stage?
